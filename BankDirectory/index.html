Purpose of this Application

This application was developed by VIGNESH R to serve as a comprehensive, real-time Online Bank Directory Search System. It empowers users, financial institutions, businesses, and bank customers to instantaneously search, locate, and verify IFSC codes, MICR codes, branch addresses, and contact details across more than 100,000 bank branches nationwide with live data synchronization, bookmarking, and Google Maps integration.
